SPECTRUM logo concepts
Four directions, three concepts each. Light and dark lockups; small icon shown at true 32 px. Earth (#AB6638) marks West Antarctica throughout.
1 Prism: warming splits into calibrated lanes
A · Split. Warming beam splits into seven blue lanes; the Earth lane for West Antarctica enters from above, independent of emissions. Agent's pick.
B · Converge. Eight lanes merge into one rising sea-level curve. At 32 px it reads as a swoosh or check mark.
C · Ribbon S. Eight parallel arcs form an S. Graduated concentric arcs sit close to the Arete badge motif.
2 Wordmark: the name is the logo
A · Rising waterline. A rising cut through the letters; light above, deep blue below (Arete's light-top, dark-bottom rule). Does not show the eight components.
B · Eight-band letters. Every letter filled with eight component bands plus an Earth cap. Bands blur into a gradient at 32 px.
C · Filled U. Solid navy word; the U fills like a container with eight bands, Earth at the fill line. Agent's pick.
3 Data glyph: the shape of the result
A · Strata. Component layers rise to a 2025 hinge, then open into a likely band and an Earth upper tail.
B · Hinge. Observed line to a 2025 hinge, then two forecast modes. Agent's pick. Fan widths need fixing: the fast West Antarctic mode should be the higher, wider one.
C · Column. Seven calibrated bars capped by a flared Earth tail. The eight bars cannot be counted at 32 px.
4 Physical emblem: the system itself
A · Grounding line. Ice on an Earth bed with a floating shelf, in a ring. Shelf unreadable at 32 px; a blue ring is close in spirit to the Arete badge.
B · Tide staff. A tide staff whose eight graduations are the components; an Earth marker points to a projected level. Agent's pick; most legible at 32 px.
C · Octagon. Eight sectors, one per component, West Antarctica pulled out. Reads as a pinwheel at 32 px.
